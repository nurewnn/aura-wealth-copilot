# Aura (`aura.social`) — Financial Clarity Feed

**Live Demo:** [https://nurewnn.github.io/aura-wealth-copilot/](https://nurewnn.github.io/aura-wealth-copilot/)  
**Challenge:** 01 — *Explain the Product to a New Visitor*

---

## Overview

First-time visitors leave traditional financial websites within seconds because they are greeted with intimidating charts, dense disclaimers, and Wall Street jargon instead of finding out whether the product actually fits their life.

**Aura (`aura.social`)** reimagines the financial welcome page as a familiar, zero-friction **Apple-style dark-glass social feed**. Instead of reading corporate paragraphs, a new visitor understands the product in under 60 seconds through interactive stories, plain-English comparisons, a live paycheck simulator, and a zero-login action launchpad.

---

## How It Solves Challenge 01

### 1. What the Product Does (`01 / What It Does`)
- **Plain-English Value Proposition:** Aura is an AI money copilot that automatically splits incoming money into locked bills, a liquid emergency buffer, and one guilt-free **Daily Safe-to-Spend** number.
- **Interactive Jargon Translator:** Visitors can toggle between *Emergency Fund*, *Paycheck Split*, and *Micro-Investing* to compare confusing bank language against Aura's one-sentence plain-English guidance.
- **60-Second Story Highlights:** Instagram/Threads-style top story rings let visitors tap through visual 15-second walkthroughs.

### 2. Who It Helps (`02 / Who It Helps`)
- **3 Real-Life Persona Switchers:** Built for **University Students** (Amir, 21), **First Full-Time Paycheck Earners** (Siti, 24), and **Freelancers & Side Hustlers** (Haziq, 26).
- **Live Monthly Cash-Flow Slider (`RM 500` – `RM 10,000`):** Visitors drag the slider directly inside the feed to see their personalized allocation across *Bills & Needs*, *Daily Spend*, *Safety Vault (3.6% p.a.)*, and *1st Global ETF Investment*, simultaneously updating the live **Apple Titanium Wallet Card** in the right sidebar.

### 3. What the Visitor Can Do Next (`03 / What To Do Next`)
- **Zero-Login Action Launchpad:** Visitors can pick one immediate micro-action right in the feed:
  1. **Build My First RM 1,000 Safety Net**
  2. **Lock My Daily Safe-to-Spend Number**
  3. **Test-Drive a RM 10 Global Index Portfolio**
- **Instant 7-Day Starter Thread & Live Copilot DM:** Selecting any path generates a personalized 7-day action plan (with one-click copy to notes), alongside an interactive **Ask Aura Copilot** DM sidebar for instant plain-English answers.
